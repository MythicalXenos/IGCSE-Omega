Paperlords IGCSE archive bundle
Retrieved: 2026-10-04

Scope: all active records returned by the Paperlords IGCSE archive for Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Economics (4EC1), Business Studies (4BS1), and English Language B (4EB1) at retrieval time.
Files: 216 PDFs total — 103 question papers, 103 mark schemes, 10 examiner reports. R papers are included wherever listed.
Folders are organized by subject and Paperlords session label. The manifest.csv lists each direct source URL and archive path.

Coverage note: this is every active PDF record listed for these three subjects in Paperlords at retrieval time, not a claim that Paperlords lists every Pearson paper ever issued. Some exam sessions may not be present in the site archive.
Use: attempt the QP under timed conditions before checking its mark scheme.
Archive index: https://www.paperlords.org/igcse
