## Manifest

`Whole_Bank_Question_Manifest.csv` has one row per parsed question-part appearance (1,022 rows), including the exact session/variant/question reference, extracted prompt, marks, available MS text, local QP/MS/ER paths, archive source URLs when registered, and provenance/extraction caveats. It deliberately keeps every sitting occurrence and does not merge similar wording. Most topic, subtopic, reasoning-family, command-word and source-check fields remain explicitly pending. Five keyed examples are reviewed so far: three P1 price-elasticity reasoning items and the two documented P2 leakage corrections. Their source text, specific skill family and item match are recorded; the two P2 rows show `QP_AND_MS_ITEM_TEXT_MATCH_CHECKED; VISUAL_PAGE_REVIEW_PENDING`, and May-June 2020 P2 also retains the session-identity conflict. All other rows have appropriate pending values, not inherited claims from another workflow. Rows sourced from the PDF text parser, especially unreviewed Paper 2 items, may contain layout spillover or omit diagram-only content. The manifest is a working evidence ledger, not a final classification or answer-quality claim.

`build_whole_bank_manifest.py` regenerates the CSV using repository-relative paths and the exact dependency pins in `requirements.txt`. Corrections/classifications belong in `Whole_Bank_Review_Layer.json`; paper identities, aliases and unresolved conflicts belong in `Whole_Bank_Paper_Identity.json`. A rebuild preserves those keyed fields, retains parser text separately in `raw_prompt_text`, and emits curated `prompt_text`/`stimulus_text`. `validate_whole_bank.py` checks unique IDs, per-paper marks, source paths, QP key/mark correspondence, local MS key coverage (with the documented (c)-only exception), persistent annotations and the two known leakage regressions. It derives inventory counts from current inputs rather than fixing the initial global totals. It does not establish visual source review or answer quality.
