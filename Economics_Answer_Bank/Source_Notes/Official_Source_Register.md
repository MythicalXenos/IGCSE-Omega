## 2. Pearson official, free, not in the repo

### 2.1 Specification — Issue 3 (Feb 2026)
`https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20GCSE/Economics/2017/Specification%20and%20SAMS/international-gcse-spec-9781446942789.pdf`

- Full subject content, Paper 1 and Paper 2, itemised by sub-topic.
- Appendix 2 = **command word taxonomy** (the official definitions).
- Appendix 3 = quantitative skills (the calculation types that can appear).
- Appendix 5 = glossary of every term the spec names.
- Issue 3 changes: January series removed, November added; onscreen assessment
  now available.

### 2.3 Assessment support page
`https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/news-policy/subject-updates/economics/international-gcse-economics-assessmentsupport.html`

This assessment-support page contains older wording that says past papers,
mark schemes and examiner reports are restricted for **nine months**. Use the
newer Qualification and Assessment Publication Policy and June 2026 Economics
update in §2.4 for the current **12-month** teacher-only access period. The page
also says:
- the ERs section contains "advice from the senior examiners on how to respond
  to each of the command verbs";
- 'R' papers are the different-time-zone variants — "an extra set of past exam
  papers you can use for mocks";
- new mark schemes are released 6–8 weeks after a series.

### 2.4 Examiner-report access policy and release notices

- Pearson Qualification and Assessment Publication Policy:
  `https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/Support/policies-for-centres-learners-and-employees/qualification-assessment-publication-policy.pdf`
- Pearson June 2026 Economics update (International GCSE reports scheduled for
  25 August 2026; confirms the 12-month teacher-only access period):
  `https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/news-policy/subject-updates/economics/june-2026-economics-update1.html`
- Pearson December 2025 Economics update (November 2025 International GCSE
  reports scheduled for 27 January 2026):
  `https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/news-policy/subject-updates/economics/december-2025-economics-update.html`

These release notices are series-level schedules, not proof that a particular
4EC1/01 or 01R report is publicly accessible. See the retrieval guide for the
current search status and caveats.
