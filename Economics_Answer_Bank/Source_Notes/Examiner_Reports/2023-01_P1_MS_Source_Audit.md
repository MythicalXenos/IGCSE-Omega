## Publication-code discrepancy — preserve literally

The standard P1 MS PDF’s URL contains `4ec1-01-rms`, and its cover identifies
Paper 01, but the printed publication code says `4EC1_02_MS_2301`. The same
PDF prints QP log `P71555A`, and its contents are the January 2023 standard P1
items. These independent identifiers support classifying this source as the
standard P1 MS while preserving the literal printed code as a discrepancy. Do
not silently “correct” the cover code to `4EC1_01_MS_2301` or reclassify the
source as Paper 2.

This is distinct from the P1R MS: its cover says Paper 01R and its QP log is
`P71556A`, while its literal publication code is `4EC1_01_MS_2301` (without R).
The QP logs and question content distinguish the two January 2023 papers even
though the MS publication codes are anomalous.

The official standard-P1 MS was read remotely and is therefore **identified**,
not locally held. Search/catalog results or inability to save the PDF locally do
not establish that it was not issued.
