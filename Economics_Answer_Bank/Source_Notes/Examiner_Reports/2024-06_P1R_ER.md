## General examiner guidance

Read the full question, including rounding requirements. Use data as evidence in economic reasoning rather than simply repeating it. “Analyse” questions do not require evaluation; “assess” requires a balanced, applied argument but not a judgement; “evaluate” requires a balanced argument and a supported conclusion.
