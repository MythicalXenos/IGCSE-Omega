# June 2021 — 4EC1 Paper 1 / Paper 1R source audit

Checked 2026-11-10. Keep the standard and regional variant evidence separate,
and distinguish the timetable, the cancellation decision, and extant documents.

## Administration status: May/June 2021 exams were cancelled

- Pearson’s 3 February 2021 announcement says that International GCSE exams
  would **not go ahead as planned** in May/June 2021 and that an additional
  International GCSE exam series would be offered later that year:
  `https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/campaigns/summer-2021-exam-support.html`
- The British Council’s contemporaneous update states explicitly that Pearson
  cancelled International GCSE exams globally and that exams were **not being
  sat in any country**. Students were instead to receive Teacher Assessed
  Grades:
  `https://www.britishcouncil.org.bd/sites/default/files/update_on_pearson_edexcel_-_mayjune_2021_series_0.pdf`
- Pearson’s March 2021 Economics update reiterates that the alternative,
  teacher-assessment approach applied to International GCSE Economics and that
  exams would not go ahead:
  `https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/news-policy/subject-updates/economics/March-2021-economics-update.html`

**Conclusion:** Neither 4EC1/01 nor 4EC1/01R was administered as a Pearson
May/June 2021 live examination. A planned timetable entry must not be reported
as a confirmed sitting. The cancellation decision is stronger evidence than
the earlier timetable.

## Regional Paper 1R (4EC1/01R): scheduled, then cancelled; documents not located

- Pearson’s final June 2021 **R Paper** timetable lists Economics 4EC1/01R,
  Paper 1R, for Thursday 20 May 2021, afternoon, 1 hour 30 minutes. The Pearson
  timetable PDF is hosted by the British Council:
  `https://www.britishcouncil.ae/sites/default/files/igcser_paper_may-june_2021_timetable.pdf`
- This establishes that the 01R exam was scheduled before cancellation. It
  does **not** establish that it took place. Pearson and the British Council’s
  cancellation notices establish that the May/June International GCSE exams
  did not go ahead.
- No exact June 2021 01R QP, MS or ER was located in the local archive or in
  the current official-Pearson search. Targeted web searches tested the paper
  and session strings `4EC1/01R`, `4EC1_01R_2106`, and the candidate QP/MS
  filenames `4EC1_01R_que_20210426`, `4EC1_01R_rms_20210604`,
  `4ec1-01r-que-20210426`, and `4ec1-01r-rms-20210604`; no June 2021 R source
  surfaced. Log-number variants `P65897A` and `P65896RA` also surfaced no

  not June 2021 P1R: Pearson’s PDF identifies it as Paper `4EC1/01` and the

## Other evidence and limits

- Pearson’s April 2021 update confirms that new unseen International GCSE
  materials were sent for TAG evidence and links an International GCSE
  April/May 2021 Unseen Content Assessment Schedule. Pearson’s direct schedule
  PDF endpoint currently returns a not-found page, and the indexed text found
  here does not establish whether a specific June P1R document was assigned as
  an unseen assessment:
  `https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/Support/Examination-timetables-for-Edexcel-International-GCSE/Apr_May_2021_FINAL_Int_GCSE.pdf`
- Pearson’s assessment-support page describes R papers as separate papers for
  different time zones and currently lists no May 2021 R grade-boundary row:
  `https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/news-policy/subject-updates/economics/international-gcse-economics-assessmentsupport.html`
  Its grade-statistics material reports a May 2021 4EC1 cohort, but those are
  qualification outcomes and do not establish that an examination paper was
  sat; Pearson’s cancellation notices identify the alternative as Teacher
  Assessed Grades.
- Pearson’s final timetable is scheduling evidence only. The cancellation
  announcement controls the administration finding. Existing QP/MS files are
  evidence of document availability only. Catalogue omissions and guessed URL
  failures are not evidence of non-issuance.

## Finding to carry forward

- **May/June 2021 administration:** cancelled; Pearson/British Council say the
  International GCSE exams were not sat globally.
- **Standard P1:** QP/MS documents exist and are locally held; the actual live
  sitting was cancelled; ER remains **not located**.
- **P1R:** scheduled on the final timetable before cancellation; the live
  sitting was cancelled; exact QP/MS/ER remain **not located**.

Do not describe either paper as administered. Do not confuse a prepared or
released paper document with an examination that candidates actually sat, and
do not upgrade an unlocated ER or R-paper document to “not issued.”
