## Whole-paper examiner advice

The report’s summary (PDF p.7) emphasizes reading the complete prompt, following specified precision, and using QP evidence as application rather than copying it. Quantitative skills may be tested through calculations, diagrams/graphs and use of extract data. Analyse is one-sided development; assess and evaluate need balance, with a judgement/conclusion specifically required for evaluate. These are report-level reminders, not replacements for the item-specific MS or command-word guidance.
