## General examiner guidance

Use evidence from the extract in causal reasoning rather than copying it. Read the whole question, especially calculation rounding and command words. “Analyse” does not require evaluation; “assess” calls for a balanced, applied argument; “evaluate” requires both sides and a supported conclusion. Quantitative skills may appear in diagrams, calculations and use of extract data (report p.54).
