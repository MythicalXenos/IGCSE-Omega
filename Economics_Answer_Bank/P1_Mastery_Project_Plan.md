## Working rules

1. **Authority order:** exact-session Pearson specification, question paper, mark scheme, examiner report and Pearson exemplar; other Pearson teaching/assessment material; credible external teaching sources; student/teacher accounts as anecdotal leads only. When sources conflict, test the claim against the exact paper and MS, document the conflict and do not launder a third-party claim into a Pearson rule.
2. **Exactness:** preserve paper/session/reference, QP log, printed publication code, page/part, marks, AO/descriptor and source URL/path. A file name is not proof of document identity. “Not found” is not “not issued.”
3. **Answer labels:** `VERIFIED CANDIDATE EXEMPLAR` only with a traceable real scored response and exact score/locator; `OFFICIAL MARK-SCHEME CONTENT` for Pearson indicative content/accepted answers; `CONSTRUCTED HIGH-MARK TARGET` for original answers. Do not claim a constructed answer is examiner-marked or guaranteed perfect. Use challenge review (“devil’s advocate”) against the exact question, mark scheme, counterargument and evidence.
4. **Scans/handwriting:** inspect page images at normal and enlarged scale, compare OCR to the pixels, repeat uncertain readings, and log uncertain words. Never repair illegible wording into a purported authentic answer. Ask the learner only if an uncertain handwritten word would change a mark.
5. **Scope/storage:** preserve source files; avoid redundant copies and unrelated staging. English/business materials are not to be deleted merely because this project is Economics. A file may be removed only after its identity, relevance, redundancy and replacement are verified. The current task authorizes a separate P2/P2R classification; do not mix its recurrence counts into P1. P2 answer construction/teaching remains a separate authorization.
6. **Copyright/evidence:** cite exact source and locator; prefer concise paraphrase and original worked answers over reproducing whole copyrighted scripts or papers.
