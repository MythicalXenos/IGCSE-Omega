### Accuracy and marking cautions from this sample

- On Q1(d), accept only an IKEA-context reason; this is an application requirement, not just a definition of induction.
- On Q4(a), 13.95% is calculated relative to 2019 inventory (4.3). Using 3.7 as denominator would answer a different question. The printed task requests two decimal places.
- On Q2(c), the official mark scheme explicitly says a definition does not score and an unexplained list is limited. Avoid presenting a generic “three points” recipe as a universal rule; this is the criterion for this item.
- For Q3(d), the question-specific AO2/AO3 split and absence of knowledge marks are from this mark scheme/report, not a blanket statement about every analyse question.
- For Q3(e), the selected option needs reasons and evaluation; the 5/9 report commentary is direct evidence of what limited the candidate. No 9/9 candidate was checked in this sample.
- For Q4(c), the official 10/12 script is a near-full-mark example with limited judgements. The extracted OCR of the QP/MS can contain layout artifacts; refer to the retained PDFs for exact typography. The existing image-based transcription in `Perfect_Answers/02_Verified_Exemplars.md` is not newly re-transcribed here.

## Official Pearson paper-based SAM (reviewed online; not retained locally)

Pearson source: [International GCSE Business Studies Sample Assessment Materials](https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20GCSE/Business%20Studies/2017/specification-and-sample-assessment/9781446942758_Int_GCSE_Bus_SAMs.pdf). Pearson’s contents identify P2 beginning at printed p.35 and the P2 mark scheme at printed p.51. The PDF was read through the fetched page extraction in this session; the source file itself was **not downloaded into the repository**, and the extraction has layout/checkbox/OCR artifacts. This limitation is retained explicitly; do not quote OCR as an exact facsimile or call the SAM locally held.

Sampled SAM P2 scope included business objectives/ownership/sectors, location and expansion, recruitment/motivation, break-even/costs, liquidity ratios, interest rates/exchange rates, financial documents and evaluation. It is a 2016/Issue 1-era benchmark for the specification introduced in 2017, not a current-specification coverage proof. The full question and mark scheme were not audited line by line against the February 2026 specification. Items should be retained for practice only where the current spec still supports them.

Calculation examples checked from the extracted SAM text (examples of arithmetic reviewed, not candidate scripts):

- Cash closing balance: 1,200,000 + 1,400,000 − 2,800,000 = **−200,000 Tks**.
- Currency conversion: 20 × 620 = **12,400 rupees**; at 1 Tks = 0.8 rupees, payment is **15,500 Tks**.
- Operating profit margin: (16m − 7m − 5m) ÷ 16m × 100 = **25%**.
- Cost example: 20,000 + 10 × (250 + 110 + 40) = **24,000 Tks**.
- A 20% rise on 11m gives **13.2m**.

These calculations were checked for arithmetic from extracted text; not all original page images have been visually checked. The OCR contains at least one typo in a formula heading and layout noise. Do not reproduce wording from the OCR as a verbatim Pearson quotation. The official SAM URL is an external source, not evidence that the repository contains the PDF.
