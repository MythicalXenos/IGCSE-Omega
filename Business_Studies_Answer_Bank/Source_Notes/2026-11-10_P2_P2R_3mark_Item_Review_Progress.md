## Review standard

For every item, confirm the actual QP prompt/task/beneficiary against the same-session MS, inspect the official MS criteria/content route(s), compare the complete linked bank answer and its attached guidance, and record any report commentary separately from the bank author's interpretation. Explain is **one relevant fact/point plus two relevant expansions**. Those expansions may form a sequence or develop the original point in separate relevant ways. Do not impose a fixed sentence count, mechanism→profit chain, context requirement, opening definition or judgement. Preserve the exact prompt's reason/advantage/disadvantage/method/purpose/effect and beneficiary. Keep official MS content, awarded scripts and constructed models distinct.
