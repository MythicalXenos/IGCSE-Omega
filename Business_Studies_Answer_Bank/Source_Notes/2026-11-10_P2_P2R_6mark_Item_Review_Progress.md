Distinguish exact QP text from MS illustrations and paraphrases. Indicative content is not an exhaustive list. Answer requested scope (including requested number of benefits/problems); do not import AO4 judgement into six-mark Analyse questions. Contextual knowledge may support reasoning without becoming a separate AO1 requirement. Local absence of an ER is recorded as “not held in reviewed folder,” not proof Pearson did not publish one.
