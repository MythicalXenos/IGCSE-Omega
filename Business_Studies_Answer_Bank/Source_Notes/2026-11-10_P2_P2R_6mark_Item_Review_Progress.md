## Review standard

For each item, check the printed QP prompt and relevant case/data context; same-session MS prompt, AO split, indicative content and level descriptors; available local ER/candidate exemplar; and the complete linked answer-bank model and attached teaching guidance. Distinguish exact QP text from MS illustrations and paraphrases. Indicative content is not an exhaustive list. Judge holistic AO2 application and AO3 analysis against the item’s descriptors, not chain/paragraph/sentence/connective quotas. Answer requested scope (including requested number of benefits/problems); do not import AO4 judgement into six-mark Analyse questions. Contextual knowledge may support reasoning without becoming a separate AO1 requirement. Local absence of an ER is recorded as “not held in reviewed folder,” not proof Pearson did not publish one.
