# 4BS1/02 Business Paper 2 — source and preparation-bank audit

**Audit snapshot:** 10 November 2026 (UTC)  
**Verdict:** **Yes — an essential preparation-resource gap remains.** The repository has broad indexed practice and a useful collection of official materials, but no fully source-verified P2 preparation bank has been established by this audit. Do not represent the answer bank as comprehensively reviewed, fully verified, or a readiness measure.

## 1. Scope, evidence standard and limits

Paper 2 (4BS1/02) is the whole Business specification examined in contexts of large businesses (Pearson describes these as businesses with more than 250 employees); it is not a separate P2-only topic list. This audit uses Pearson’s **International GCSE Business Specification, Issue 2, February 2026**, 41 pages, retained at [`Sources/Official_Pearson/International_GCSE_Business_Specification_Issue2_Feb2026.pdf`](Sources/Official_Pearson/International_GCSE_Business_Specification_Issue2_Feb2026.pdf). The qualification and assessment appendices were read. P2 is 90 minutes, 80 marks, 50% of the qualification, one tier, with a calculator permitted. The specification states each paper assesses the whole specification; P2 investigates large businesses.

Evidence labels used here:

- **Held/readable:** a local PDF opens and has readable text; this does not prove it was fully read.
- **Identity checked:** paper code/session on the source itself was compared; a filename or archive pairing alone is not enough.
- **Sample checked:** specified source pages/items were compared. This is not full-document review.
- **Verified example:** exact prompt, question-paper page, mark-scheme criteria and, where applicable, Pearson’s printed candidate mark/comment were compared.
- **Indexed / bank claim:** a register or generated answer exists; not source verification.
- **Not established:** no proof of absence or completeness. A failed guessed URL, absent third-party listing or blank archive field is not proof that Pearson did not publish a resource.

This is a bounded handoff audit, not a line-by-line content audit of every answer or every archived document. Files saying “verified”, “complete” or “full marks” are not treated as evidence without independent source checks. No learner knowledge or readiness has been assessed.

### Tariffs, command words and assessment demands

- The specification’s command taxonomy is authoritative (Appendix 2). It defines **Define, Give, State, Identify, Calculate, Complete the table, Outline, Explain, Analyse, Justify, Evaluate** and MCQ. These are not interchangeable. `State` and `Outline` require context; `Explain` is expressly no-context in the specification; `Analyse`, `Justify` and `Evaluate` each have different required task structures.
- Review and teach tariffs in the requested order **12 → 9 → 6 → 3 → 2 → 1**. Families should be grouped by recurring reasoning (e.g., motivation options, financing choice, market-research use), not only identical wording; each prompt still needs its own case/AO check. The bank has material labelled across tariffs, but family-level prompt coverage and every point/mark are not independently reconciled.

- Paper 2 assesses AO1 knowledge, AO2 application, AO3 analysis and AO4 evaluation. Specification weightings: AO1 27–28%, AO2 38–39%, AO3 22–23%, AO4 11–12%; no more than 15% of total marks tests knowledge recall. These are whole-qualification figures, not a guarantee of per-paper allocation.
- P2 total: 80 marks. ## 5. Substantive findings and defects to correct

1. **The bank is not an authoritative “perfect answers / verified” source by default.** Its front-page claim that 252 answers cover every governed question and that 28 checks verify everything exceeds what those checks establish. Structural check passes are not substantive checks of source identity, calculations, marking logic, wording, application or supported judgement. Keep the scripts as navigation/triage, not quality certification.
2. **Answer fidelity/extraction problems exist.** Existing human/automated triage reported 23 quoted stems not found in QP/MS text layers, four paraphrases and further fragments/no quotes; one P1R archive pair is an apparent mismatch. Many flags may be caused by extraction, not confirmed errors. Repair only after comparing source pages; do not bulk-delete/rewrite from noisy OCR/text-layer output. Keep the May/June 2020 P2 identity mismatch above as a confirmed cover discrepancy.
3. **AO/marking coverage is not demonstrated.** The existing MS-point comparison found 18 apparent AO coverage gaps among a bounded 159 entries; this is a triage result that needs human inspection. A model must not omit an AO tested by the question, but the criterion must be read from that question’s MS and appropriate level descriptors. Do not turn indicative bullets into mandatory wording/quotas.
4. **Calculation checks require source-first repair.** The current `audit_calculations.py` produces false positives by regex/parsing arithmetic in MS distractors (e.g., May 2023 P2’s correct distractor calculation for 13.64% was misparsed; May 2024 P2R `2 + 5 + 7 = 15.0` was flagged though the left-hand numbers may be separated by extraction/table structure). Do not change source figures or answer files from those alerts alone. The May/June 2020 P2R file’s cover mismatch makes its three arithmetic alerts especially unsafe to teach until the source is settled. A human paper-by-paper numeric audit remains open.
5. **Examiner guidance overgeneralisation:** examiner commentary is context-specific. Remove unsupported paragraph/chain quotas, required opening definitions, score promises, rigid “must mention” formulas and universal writing prohibitions. A concise response can meet a criterion; a definition is not automatically required unless the question demands it. Justify is recommendation between options; evaluate requires a supported conclusion on the stated situation. Use the MS’s AO split and level descriptors, not Economics rules.
6. **Candidate sample classifications need a source pass.** Keep Pearson-scored script, official indicative answer and constructed model labels separate. The May 2023 P2 Q4(c) example is Pearson-awarded 10/12, not full marks; the official MS has indicative AO content; any fresh exemplar prose is constructed and unscored.
7. **Corrections made in this handoff:** root and Perfect Answers READMEs, `ACCURACY.md`, and `00_Pearson_Official_Rulebook.md` now state the audit limit and defer to the current specification/item-specific MS. `01_Mark_Tariff_Playbook.md` now reproduces the current command taxonomy and retracts fixed-answer-shape/AO claims. `10_Chain_Bank.md`, `11_Self_Marking_Protocol.md`, `02_Verified_Exemplars.md`, and the 2023 P2/P2R exemplar commentary withdraw the fixed sentence/paragraph/word quotas, compulsory definition openings, universal context/bullet rules and unsupported prediction language identified during this review. These are targeted corrections, not a complete prose/source audit of those files.
8. **Exhaustiveness and frequency:** scripts report 38 papers, 988 items, 3040 marks and 380 extended-response blocks, but those totals do not prove all sessions, all item families, all spec points or source IDs are correct. No frequency ranking should be derived from this or a partial sample. Preserve uncommon content and question families.

## 6. Essential gap verdict and next actions

**Essential preparation gap: YES.** The specification and substantial practice inventory exist; a learner or teacher can find practice, but cannot yet rely on the full bank as a primary-source-verified preparation resource. Material risks are (i) the mislabelled 2020 MS pair, (ii) incomplete QP/MS/ER/SAM source identity and access map, (iii) unreviewed topic and tariff coverage, and (iv) substantive answer/calculation/marking issues that structural validators cannot rule out. This is a resource-quality verdict only, not a judgement about any student’s preparation.

Prioritised completion:

1. Resolve both 2020 QP/MS session discrepancies from full printed covers/content and official publication/history; until then mark the pair **identity unresolved** and exclude its MS as an authoritative teaching match.
2. Validate all 19 P2/P2R pairs visually and against question-by-question order/case, item count and printed tariff total; separately record held/readable/reviewed/verified and the matching ER/SAM/exemplar status. Do not infer missing reports from empty register cells.
3. Locate the current official SAM and complete the Pearson resource/access matrix, respecting centre-only restriction and release timing. Reconcile local ER files with official qualification-page listings; read each P2/P2R report that is claimed as source evidence.
4. Generate a per-question source trace (QP prompt/page/mark ↔ matching MS prompt/AO/tariff/answer ↔ ER page/score if present) and validate all P2 per-paper item totals = 80. Review tariff families in **12 → 9 → 6 → 3 → 2 → 1** order, while keeping all rare prompt families.
5. Human-review every answer retained as verified: arithmetic and units, MS provision of formulae, transcription against page images, accepted alternatives, AO/level logic, case application and conclusion. Correct extraction contamination and duplicates only with source evidence; preserve useful annotations and change history.
6. Complete the subsection and quantitative-skill crosswalk against checked, not merely indexed, resources; add learning/practice materials for gaps. Independently verify claims in legacy coverage/completion files, narrow their language, then rerun build scripts and structural checks as secondary validation.
7. Keep official scripts, official indicative content and constructed examples distinct. Add exact series/paper/question/page and mark source to every retained example; avoid any claimed mark guarantee.
