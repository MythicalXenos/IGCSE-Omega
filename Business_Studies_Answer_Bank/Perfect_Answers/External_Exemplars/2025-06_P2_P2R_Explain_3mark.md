## May–June 2025 P2R

### Q1(f) — reason an employer might use a letter; printed p.8

**QP task:** Explain one reason why an employer might use a letter to communicate with an employee. **Matched MS routes:** letters are private for communicating a job offer, promotion, suspension or redundancy to confirm the next step; or formal communication can portray a professional business image and be personal to an employee.

> **[Candidate-script transcription, visually checked against printed p.8.]**
> *“A letter may be used for communication by an employer to discuss state some sensitive/personal information such as a formal letter that states the dismissal to the employee and it may not be appropriate to say it on text or other forms of communication.”*

**Examiner comment:** candidate identifies sensitive/personal information and develops why text or other communication may not be appropriate; the examiner says there is no further development for the third mark. This is the examiner's evaluation of this response, not a rule that every acceptable answer must cite privacy.

### Q2(c) — one reason some businesses fail; printed p.15

**QP task:** Explain one reason why some businesses fail. **Matched MS routes:** poor cash flow → lack of finance → inability to pay suppliers/other debtors; or prices too high/not competitive → demand falls → revenue is lacking.

> **[Candidate-script transcription, visually checked against printed p.15.]**
> *“Business fail because them fail to innovate. This can lead to new trends in the market and the business are using the old product that are no longer what people are looking for. This will result in the product to decline and profits to decrease.”*

**Examiner comment:** awarded 3 marks; the examiner identifies the reason as failure to innovate, followed by old products no longer being what people seek, then product/profit decline. This Pearson-awarded route is separate from the MS examples.

### Q2(d) — reason to use targeted online advertising; printed p.16

**QP task:** Explain one reason why a business would use targeted advertising online to promote itself. **Matched MS routes:** send information to past customers → greater likelihood of repeat purchase → improved loyalty; or target a specific demographic → use different online activities → increased brand awareness.

> **[Candidate-script transcription, visually checked against printed p.16.]**
> *“Targeted advertising saves on costs for marketing as it specifically targets people who want or need their products so there is a guarantee that they will buy from the business. As the business is more likely to make sales. They can choose to target a specific demographic based on income or lifestyle to increase their chances. Like Rabanne would target people who have an interest in its products based on past purchases.”*

**Examiner comment:** awarded 3 marks. The examiner describes the credited development as the particular audience being more likely to buy and then make more sales. The candidate's word “guarantee” is not converted into a universal factual claim or an MS requirement.

### Q2(e) — reason to use statements of comprehensive income in decisions; printed p.18

**QP task:** Explain one reason why businesses use statements of comprehensive income when making decisions. **Matched MS routes:** see business performance → calculate profit margin → use other calculations if performance has deteriorated; or compare with similar businesses → judge performance against competitors → assess how well the business is doing.

**Examiner's summary of the 3/3 response:** one reason was that the statement “shows the profits,” developed to identify “where costs are too high,” and then to decide to lower costs/increase sales. **Examiner tip:** only two further marks are available for developing the reason; longer responses are not necessary. These are examiner quotations/summary, not a verbatim candidate-script transcription.

## May–June 2025 P2

### Q1(f) — disadvantage of training employees; printed p.8

**QP task:** Explain one disadvantage for a business of training its employees. **Matched MS routes:** training takes time and uses existing employees → may reduce business productivity; or existing employees must be taught to train others → extra expense → no guarantee they train efficiently.

> **[Candidate-script transcription, visually checked against printed p.8.]**
> *“One disadvantage for the business may be that when a business provides training for the new employees. After some time employees may leave and join rival business, and this is why business gets frustrated, that is why this is a disadvantage for business training its employees.”*

**Examiner comment:** the response gained one mark. The candidate identified that the trained employee may join a rival, but did not develop the point to show consequences for the business. This does not establish that a developed staff-departure route can never be relevant.

### Q2(c) — reason to use JIT; printed p.14

**QP task:** Explain one reason why many manufacturing businesses use JIT. **Matched MS routes:** reduced inventory → lower over-ordering costs → potentially lower overall costs; or less out-of-date/damaged stock → less storage needed → space available for other business activities.

> **[Candidate-script transcription, visually checked against printed p.14.]**
> *“One reason many manufacturing businesses use just-in-time because all the goods can be finished faster as all workers and machinery are deployed. This method is used when demand for certain goods is high. Ex. Toyota cars.”*

**Examiner comment:** no marks. The report says JIT does not mean goods are produced or delivered faster; it concerns inventory management/control. This is an item-specific relevance correction.

### Q2(d) — why it matters to pay suppliers on time; printed p.16

**QP task:** Explain one reason why it is important that businesses pay their suppliers on time. **Matched MS routes:** payment on time establishes a good supplier relationship → if goods are urgently needed, suppliers may be more likely to help; or better negotiating power → possibly better prices → lower costs.

> **[Candidate-script transcription, visually checked against printed p.16.]**
> *“Paying suppliers on time establishes a healthy business-supplier relationship. Hence, more discounts may be provided by suppliers. Therefore, decreasing the costs of raw materials.”*

**Examiner comment:** this accurate and concise response gained all three marks; the examiner describes the relationship point plus two developments. The script is a Pearson-awarded example, not the MS wording.

### Q2(e) — effect on a business of decreased interest rates; printed p.17

**QP task:** Explain one effect on a business if interest rates are decreased. **Matched MS routes:** lower business borrowing costs → cheaper financing for projects/expansion → improved profitability; or higher consumer spending → consumers buy additional items → higher sales income.

> **[Candidate-script transcription, visually checked against printed p.17.]**
> *“If interest rates are decreased, business will be more likely to take out bank loans. This might have a positive or a negative impact on businesses, depending how they handle debt and repaying.”*

**Examiner comment:** the response gives a valid point about being more likely to take out loans, but does not develop the consequences for the business. The ER also notes accepted routes through lower borrowing costs or consumers having more disposable income. The candidate response is not treated as an MS route.
