# Q1(b) — Define *break-even* — 1 mark

### ❌ 0 marks
> *"Break even analysis is the analysis of how many goods are needed to be produced to meet the break even point."*

**Examiner comment:** *"This response shows that the candidate is **trying to explain** what breakeven is but **it is not giving a definition**."* **Tip:** *"Aim to be specific in your response – is the response a definition or an explanation?"*

### ✅ 1 mark
> *"It happens when there is not profit nor loss. It is the point where revenue and expenses are the same which results in no profit nor loss."*

**Examiner comment:** *"A clear definition of the term break-even."*

**Report guidance:**
> *"Only a small proportion of the candidates gained the mark available. **A number [of] responses seen mentioned 'the point at which profit meets costs' which is not a definition of the term.** Candidates must ensure that they are giving a **true definition** of the term."*
> *"Candidates should also be careful to try and **avoid using the term in their response**."*

> ⭐ **Break-even, precisely:** **the level of output at which total revenue exactly equals total costs** — the business makes neither profit nor loss.
> ⚠️ **"Profit meets costs" is wrong** — profit is not a cost. **Names the two things being equal: revenue and total costs.**

# Q1(f) — Explain **one disadvantage** of questionnaires — 3 marks

### ✅ 3 marks
> *"A disadvantage of using questionnaires as a method of market research could be that customers might not be saying the truth which could cause a misunderstanding and ending up creating a product which people don't want."*

**Examiner comment:** *"This response was typical of many candidates for this question. Firstly, the candidate **wastes time by repeating the question in the first sentence**. However, the comment about **customers not telling the truth** is a valid response and this is **further developed** by commenting that this could **cause a misunderstanding and could end up creating a product people do not want**."*

**⭐ Examiner tip:** *"**Do not repeat the question in your answer. It wastes time.**"*

### ⚠️ 2 marks
*(response partly illegible in source)* — **Examiner comment:** *"This response gives a disadvantage in a slightly different way. The comment about supporting other brands could be very relevant… **However they did not develop the response further to gain the full marks.**"*

### ⭐⭐ The tip that matters most on this question
> *"**Questions that ask to explain an advantage or disadvantage need a different technique to those that ask candidates to explain a method or way.**"*

> ### 🎯 TWO DIFFERENT EXPLAIN TECHNIQUES
> | The question asks for… | Your three rungs |
> |---|---|
> | **a method / a way** | name the method → how it works → **the business outcome** |
> | **an advantage / a disadvantage** | name the effect → **why it arises** → **the consequence for the business** |
>
> **Getting these confused is why candidates "list methods" on advantage questions and "describe effects" on method questions.** Read which noun the question actually uses.

**Also:** *"Many candidates misread **disadvantage for advantage**"* · *"the question asked for one disadvantage not several"*.

# ⭐⭐ Q1(g) — ANALYSE the benefits of being a multinational — 6 marks

> **Report guidance:** *"'Analyse' questions are testing two of the Assessment Objectives… **AO2 'Application' and AO3 'Analysis'**… Candidates must be aware that **there are no marks available for knowledge**, and that the response **must be in context of LEGO**."* · *"Examiners will look at the response **'holistically'**."*

### ✅ **Level 3 – 5 marks**
> *"when Lego is a multinational there are some benefits. Multi national operate in more than one country therefore Lego will be able to have more customers as a result more profit can be earned. Also due to multi national Lego will be able to get more brand reputation therefore Lego will be able to survive in the market for long time when to Lego operates as a multinational Lego will be able to skill full labours and cheaper labours therefore quality products will be produced and Lego will have increased consumer loyalty. Also when Lego operates as a move it can get access to advance technology therefore quality production and Lego will be benefited"*

**Examiner comment:** *"This response clearly understands the benefits of LEGO being a multinational business, the reference to **business operating in more than one country and gaining more customers is relevant**. The reference again to being a multinational business allows it the opportunity of **having a wider choice of skillful employees**. This shows that there is **sound application of knowledge and business understanding**. The response then further develops the points already made by **gaining customer loyalty throughout the countries**."*

### ⚠️ **Level 2 – 4 marks**
> *"Firstly LEGO will have access to a larger market. This is because they can sell their Harry Potter Legos to many kids, girls, boys, teens around the world. Therefore will increase consumer base. As a result increases sales of the products. Hence gives LEGO a competitive edge over other rivals in the Lego and toy market.*
>
> *Secondly LEGO can purchase its raw materials like plastic to make the Lego pieces for a cheaper price. This is because LEGO can purchase it in bulk from the developing countries getting a trade discount. Therefore this will reduce the costs of the Lego toy. Hence can sell the toy for a cheaper price. As a result will grab the attention of potential consumers."*

**Examiner comment:** *"This response shows **sound application** of knowledge and understanding of business concepts. The reference to selling toys and **having a competitive edge over other rivals in the toy market** is relevant, **but not further developed as to how being a multinational could further enhance the selling of toys**. The second paragraph with reference to purchasing of raw materials and making large purchases again is a benefit of a multinational business and again **clearly shows sound knowledge**. **To gain the higher level the candidate needed to develop further the comments made and give interconnected points.**"*

> ### 🎯 5 marks vs 4 marks — and this one will surprise you
>
> **The 4-mark response is BETTER structured.** It has two clear, well-linked paragraphs, each with *"This is because… Therefore… As a result… Hence…"* — perfect connectives. It names *"Harry Potter Legos"*, *"plastic"*, *"trade discount"*, *"the toy market"*. On any normal reading it is the stronger answer.
>
> **And it scored 4.**
>
> **The examiner's reason:** *"not further developed as to how being a multinational could further enhance the selling of toys"* and *"needed to give **interconnected points**."*
>
> **The 5-mark answer rambles — one long paragraph, no connectives, near-illegible in places — but it keeps returning to business-level outcomes** (*"more profit"*, *"survive in the market for long time"*, *"increased consumer loyalty"*, *"Lego will be benefited"*).
>
> ### 🔴 THE LESSON: on Analyse, **connectives are not the same as analysis.**
> *"Therefore… as a result… hence"* chain words together, but if each link ends at a mechanism (*"cheaper price"*, *"competitive edge"*) rather than a business outcome, the examiner reads it as **description in a chain**, not analysis.
>
> **Rung check: does each chain end on the BUSINESS — profit, survival, loyalty, market position? If it ends on "a cheaper price", keep going.**
