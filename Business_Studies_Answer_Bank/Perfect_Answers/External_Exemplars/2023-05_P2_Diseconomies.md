# May 2023 P2 — diseconomies of scale, 2(d)

**Source:** Pearson May 2023 P2 examiner report, `4BS1_Business_Studies_May_2023_P2_ER.pdf`, printed pp.29–30; matched question and MS in the same-session QP/MS PDFs.

## Actual question and official MS

**QP prompt:** *“Explain one reason why diseconomies of scale may occur.”* (3)

**Official MS routes (AO1, 3 marks):**

- The business may become too large (1) → employees become demotivated (1) → productivity falls (1).
- The business relies more on bureaucracy (1) → more time is spent in administration (1) → decision-making is slower (1).

The MS says no marks are awarded for a definition on this item. It also accepts any other appropriate response. These are official MS examples, separate from the candidate script below.

## Pearson-awarded response — 3/3

> **[Candidate script transcription, visually checked against the local primary ER, printed p.30; handwriting is not available in the PDF text layer.]**
>
> *“One reason is because the business is too large or has too many employees. With an increased amount of employees communication can be difficult leading to misunderstandings and orders not getting carried out. This means that the business isn't functioning effectively, leading to diseconomies of scale.”*

The report’s score line on p.29 identifies this as a 3-mark response. Its examiner comment on p.30 says:

> *“The response includes one reason why diseconomies of scale may occur, it is then further developed by increasing communication between employees which could cause misunderstanding.”*

The examiner comment and the awarded score were also visually checked against the relevant printed pages. The script is a Pearson-awarded response, not MS content and not an author-written model. It provides one example of development; it does not prescribe a sentence count, final endpoint or wording for other Explain items.
