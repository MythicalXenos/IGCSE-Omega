### `1(a)(i)` — 1 mark (AO1:1)

**Question:** Which one of the following is an external source of finance?

**Options:** A · Personal savings · B · Retained profit · C · Bank loan · D · Selling assets

**Credited option:** **C · Bank loan**

**Official mark scheme answer:**

AO1 - 1 mark

The only correct answer is C

A is not correct because personal savings are an

internal source of finance

B is not correct because retained profit is an internal

source of finance

D is not correct because selling assets is an internal

source of finance


### `1(a)(ii)` — 1 mark (AO1:1)

**Question:** Which one of the following is the name of a pricing strategy where a business sets a high price for a new product in the market?

**Options:** A · Penetration · B · Skimming · C · Competition · D · Promotional

**Credited option:** **B · Skimming**

**Official mark scheme answer:**

AO1 - 1 mark

The only correct answer is B

A is not correct because penetration pricing is where a

business sets a low price for a new product

C is not correct because competition pricing is where a

business sets a price for a product in line with its

competitors

D is not correct because promotional pricing is where a

business lowers the price of a product for a short time
