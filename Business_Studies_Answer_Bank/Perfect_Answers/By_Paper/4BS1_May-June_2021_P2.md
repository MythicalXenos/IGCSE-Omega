## Question 1

### `1(a)(i)` — 1 mark (AO1:1)

**Question:** Which one of the following sectors would a retail outlet operate in?

**Options:** A · Primary · B · Secondary · C · Tertiary · D · Quarternary

**Credited option:** **C · Tertiary**

**Official mark scheme answer:**

AO1 - 1 mark

The only correct answer is C

A is not correct because primary is extracting raw

materials

B is not correct as secondary is the manufacturing

of the goods

D is not correct as quarternary is not a section in

retail under this word
