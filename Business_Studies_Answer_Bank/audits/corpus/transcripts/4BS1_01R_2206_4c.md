## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*A venture capitalist is an investor who puts money into a business to help it grow, and it is
long-term money.*

*For Can Café:* an investor interested in food could fund the new café, which gives it enough capital
to widen what it sells — coffee, drinks, juices — and adds a second business whose profits add to the
restaurant's. The investor benefits too if it succeeds, so the relationship stays healthy.

*Against:* the investor takes a share of the profits — including from the restaurant's own dishes — so
profit is no higher than it would have been with another source, and there is less left to spend on
training staff, which demotivates them and cuts output.

*Conclusion:* use it, because it is long-term money and it funds the café — **but** it depends how much
profit the restaurant is already making, since the investor will take a share of the new business's
profit as well. It also leaves the other business free to fund its own innovation and advertising.
