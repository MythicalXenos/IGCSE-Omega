## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*Following the market matters because the menu has to keep up with what customers want: if a dish is
popular elsewhere, this restaurant should spot the demand and copy it, which wins popularity and
revenue; if it does not, its food goes out of fashion and sales fall, and in the worst case it closes.*

*It also matters because it stops the business wasting money: expanding when a strong rival has just
entered would be pointless, so the money is better spent — a new chef who can make the trending dish in
the restaurant's own style — and responding to the change therefore lowers the risk of failure and
gives better decisions.*
