## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*Through the customer:* higher mortgage payments → less disposable income in China → fewer bicycle
tours sold → CCT cuts price to hold demand → the price may not cover costs.

*Or not:* the customer who matters may be **foreign**, holding a stronger currency than the CNY, for
whom the tours just got cheaper — so demand may not fall at all.

*Through the business:* CCT's own loans cost more → average total costs rise → profit falls → no money
to hire guides or improve the offer → no expansion to Beijing.

*Or not:* the rise is **0.2 percentage points**, which may be invisible to a customer paying for a
bike rental in Shanghai, so revenue holds and short-term debts are still covered.

*Overall:* it is a big impact, and the remedy (more advertising to reach tourists who will pay) adds
to the same liabilities the interest rise created.
