## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*Give the new chef a permanent contract.* It gives them stable pay and hours, which makes them feel
secure, which motivates them, which makes them work harder on the new desserts, which makes those
desserts better, which earns the restaurant a name for good desserts and separates it from the
restaurants nearby, which sells more and raises revenue.

*The cost:* a permanent contract is dearer and less flexible — the chef cannot be laid off or have pay
cut if trade falls, and the new desserts may not sell at all if rivals already do them well — so the
extra employment cost could outweigh the extra revenue and squeeze the margin.

*Overall:* worth it, because loyalty and lower turnover save recruitment costs — **provided** there is
demand for the desserts, so do market research first.
