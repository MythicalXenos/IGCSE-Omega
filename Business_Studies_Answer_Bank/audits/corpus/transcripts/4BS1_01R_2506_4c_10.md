## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*Through the customer:* borrowing costs more, so households borrow less and spend less → demand falls
in China → CCT sells fewer tours → revenue falls → the risk of failure rises.

*Through the business:* CCT's own investment (more bicycles, more services) costs more to finance →
production costs rise → profit falls if revenue holds → less finance for expansion.

*Or not:* the rise is small, so the effect on demand may be small; and CCT sells to **foreign**
tourists on bilingual tours, whose demand is driven by wanting to see Shanghai rather than by Chinese
interest rates.

*Overall:* the effect is probably limited — **"it depends"** on whether that foreign demand holds up.
