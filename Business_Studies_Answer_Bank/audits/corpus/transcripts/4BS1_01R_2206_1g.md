## L6 — what the candidate was saying

**Benefit:** customers order on the app → waiters spend less time taking and mis-hearing orders →
orders reach the kitchen directly → food goes out faster → more meals in the same time → productivity
and customer satisfaction rise, and those customers come back.

**Risk:** customers who dislike or cannot use the app are frustrated, spend time working it out and
leave → fewer orders → fewer meals cooked → productivity falls; and chefs who cannot work the new
system slow down too, so productivity falls again.

**This is one factor — technology — driven in both directions**, which is exactly what the examiner
praises: *"a good example of positive and negative impacts on productivity."*
