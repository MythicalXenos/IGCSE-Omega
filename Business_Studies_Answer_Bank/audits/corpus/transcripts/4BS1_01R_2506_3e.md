## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*Sell the assets, not spend the reserve.* CCT is a **small** business: it needs a cash buffer for
emergencies and it cannot raise money quickly, so the funding choice is as much about protecting the
reserve as about buying bicycles. Selling unused equipment buys the bicycles **and** keeps the
retained profit where it is.

*The cost of that choice:* assets take time to sell, then the order takes time to arrive, and in the
gap the tourists are still tired — they complain, they switch to a rival tour operator in Shanghai,
and revenue goes with them.

*Overall:* selling assets, **on condition that the sale completes fast enough that tourists are not
left waiting** — and the short-term worry (permanently losing the assets) is accepted in exchange for
a long-term reserve that covers downturns and the low season.
