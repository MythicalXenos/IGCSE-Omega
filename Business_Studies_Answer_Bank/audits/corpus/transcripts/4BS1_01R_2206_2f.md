## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*Choose breakfast.* Tourists are up early and looking for somewhere to eat, so opening for breakfast
meets a demand that already exists and raises sales — **unless** the nearby restaurants already run
breakfast buffets, in which case the advantage is competed away.

*The cost:* staff must start earlier and work longer, which demotivates them, which lowers food
quality, which loses customers — **unless** the local competition is worse, in which case this
restaurant's long-standing reputation still wins.

*Overall:* breakfast is cheaper than an advertising campaign in the short term and builds reputation
in the long term — **but** it depends on tourist numbers, and a pandemic would remove them.
