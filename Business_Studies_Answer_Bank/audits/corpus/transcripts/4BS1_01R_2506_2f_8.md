## L6 — what the candidate was saying

*For customer satisfaction:* satisfied riders come back instead of going to a rival → sales and profit
rise → the profit funds tours in other Chinese cities such as Beijing; and satisfaction produces word
of mouth, which is free advertising → customer base and market share grow in Shanghai.

*Against it:* satisfaction is hard to measure, because different tourists are satisfied by different
things → it may not be a reliable measure.

*For profit:* it satisfies the owners, who then reinvest → better bicycles → which is *also* what makes
customers satisfied.

*Against profit:* profit alone will not make the business successful, because dissatisfied riders do
not return and rivals gain the edge; and profit does not show whether the business is *growing*.

*Verdict:* customer satisfaction — because online reviews are easy to get, and they make customers
loyal on a return visit. **"However, it much depends on the objectives set by the owner for CCT."**
