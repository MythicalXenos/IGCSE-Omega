## 3. Every paper is the same 26 questions — 38 of 38 papers, no exceptions

**12 × 1-mark · 4 × 2-mark · 4 × 3-mark · 3 × 6-mark Analyse · 2 × 9-mark Justify · 1 × 12-mark Evaluate.**

So **you know the shape before you turn the page.** Three Analyses, two Justifies, one Evaluate.

| Tariff | The shape that scores | What is **NOT** in it |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Define** | what it is, in the scheme's words | the word inside its own definition — *"benefits given to employees"* is circular; say **"extra, over and above pay"** |
| **1 State** | one fact **with a noun from the case in the sentence** | a definition. **One correct answer with no context has scored 0.** |
| **2 Calculate** | working + **the answer to the stated decimal places** | a right number at the wrong d.p. — **that loses the mark** |
| **3 Explain** | cause → effect → **business outcome** | a definition. *"No marks are awarded for a definition."* |
| **6 Analyse** | **two** chains, context throughout | **any judgement — there is no AO4.** No conclusion needed. |
| **9 Justify** | choose · chains · the drawback of your choice · decision **+ condition** | knowledge of both options — *"there are no knowledge marks"* |
| **12 Evaluate** | 3/3/3/3 — both sides **and** a last word that decides | a summary of what you have just said |

## 4. The five sentence moves — copy these

**A. The 6-mark second chain** (your biggest single habit: 7 marks)
> *"Another reason is ___. This means ___, which means ___, so [the business] ___."*
Each chain must end at **money, customers, or a named department**. If both chains end at "better
reputation", you have written one chain twice.

**B. The 9-mark condition** (4 marks on your paper)
> *"I would choose ___ because ___. **This depends on** ___."*
Name a circumstance **from the case** — a number, a customer type, a product. Not *"the objectives"*.

**C. The 12-mark last word** (your 6/12 became 6 partly for this)
> *"Overall, ___ matters most because ___. **In the short term** ___; **however, in the long term** ___. **This depends on** ___."*

**D. The method that must be applied, not listed** (your 4(c) fault)
> *"[Method] means [employee] is paid ___ for ___. So they are motivated to ___, which raises ___."*
Never write a list of methods and no chain. One method chained beats five named.

**E. The category, not the generalisation** (your 1(d) risk)
> Instead of *"to evaluate their range of products"* write *"to identify the **dog** products and stop
> making them"*. **Name the tool's own term.**

## 7. Three things not to do, because someone will tell you to

- **"Always do both sides."** Not required, and not the criterion. The criterion is **evaluate vs
  describe** — one option weighed scores 9/9 (June 2019 P1 Q3(e)); two options weighed scores 9/9
  (June 2025 P2R Q2(f)). Two options *described* is Level 2.
- **"Say it depends on cost."** The phrase is worth nothing without a named condition, and a script
  that says the phrase without one scored **8/9**.
- **"Write a conclusion on every long answer."** A 6-mark Analyse has **no AO4 marks**, so a
  conclusion earns nothing there. Spend that minute on the second chain.
