People are often interested in celebrities and their lives. They follow their every move, dress like them, imitate them. Many people take more interest in celebrities and their lives than their own friends and family.

A lot of people are so enthralled by the celebrities and their public fronts that they end up neglecting their own families and friends. Celebrities' lives are crafted to look the glamorous - to look dazzling, and entertaining. No one crafts your aunt's lifestyle to be so. So, we idolize them, go their concerts, meet and greets, all to have a chance to get to see them. We watch their shows for hours and hours. Valuable time, resources, and effort that we could have otherwise spent on being there for our family and friends.

At work, gossip about colleague feels more like information, whereas, gossip about a celebrity feels like entertainment. When we are online, we see news about celebrities and all the crazy things that they do. We see them sharing their luxurious lives and want to be in their position. Our families and friends are too close and personal for us to feel that way. Celebrities' public images are artificially crafted to be the most interesting. They are tailored to draw attention. They do this for exposure. That is why celebrities' garner so much attention and interest from people.

However, this isn't the case for everyone. I, myself, make a conscious effort to make time for my friends and family. I try to be there for them whenever I can. Many other people do the same. They may simply not care for the celebrities or just prioritize their friends and families. Even then, many people still idolize celebrities and follow them. They see the glamorous front and worship it.

Most people are interested in celebrities' lives and the front they put out. They are drawn in by their carefully crafted public image.
