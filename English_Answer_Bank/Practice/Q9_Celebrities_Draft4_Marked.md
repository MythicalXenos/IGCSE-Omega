## Skeleton read as a list
1. People are often interested in celebrities and their lives.                      YES
2. A lot of people are so enthralled ... that they end up neglecting ...            YES + mechanism
3. At work, gossip about a colleague feels more like information ...                WHY, second instance

Sequence: YES, YES, WHY, EXCEPT, YES. One argument. The turn happens once, in para 4, and is

## What genuinely improved
- "No one crafts your aunt's lifestyle to be so." Short, concrete, funny, and it carries the whole
  mechanism. Best sentence in the essay.
- The workplace-gossip comparison is a NEW idea, not a repeat. Information vs entertainment is a
  distinction, and distinctions are what "perceptive" means in the mark scheme.
- "Our families and friends are too close and personal for us to feel that way." This is a real
  thought - the closeness removes the fantasy. It is under-served (see below) but it is the most
  original idea in the piece.
- Paragraph lengths are now 94 / 97 / 70. Even, and within range.

## The remaining fault: the conclusion still repeats
Sentence 2, "They are drawn in by their carefully crafted public image", is paragraph 3 sentence 4.
The conclusion now has a proportion (good) and then restates the mechanism (already known).
It still does not answer "so what". The essay's job in its last two lines is to say what follows
from everything above, and it currently says what was already said.

## Repetition of one idea, five ways
"public fronts" / "crafted to look the glamorous" / "artificially crafted to be the most
interesting" / "tailored to draw attention" / "carefully crafted public image".
One claim, five formulations, across two paragraphs and the conclusion. The examiner reads this as
the "repetitive" half of "repetitive or were brief". Fix: state the mechanism ONCE, at full
strength, and spend the other slots on consequences.
