## Evidence standard

A response enters this folder only after the handwriting has been visually checked against the official report facsimile. Each record separates the exact QP prompt from the answer text and includes the session/component/question, published score and level, printed report page(s), candidate-script page(s), and official source. Cross-outs and uncertain wording are flagged rather than silently repaired. OCR is not used as the source of exact transcription.

The authentic-response count remains **92**; the June 2023 P1 Q8, P1R Q8, P1 Q9–Q11, P1R Q9, P1R Q10 and P1R Q11 scripts are cross-linked to completed answer-set entries, and the May/June 2019 P1 Q8 answer set is constructed (its two authentic Q8 responses stay locator-only and add no transcript here) (P1R Q9 was re-read from rendered crops of ER pp. 40–43 and its transcript corrected: “As I once Mansa Musa exclaimed”, “such as planet” with an unclear extra stroke, “an ave person”, “paniced”, “inorder”, “poull”, and the struck-word records); P1R Q10 was re-read from rendered crops of ER pp. 47–49, resolving “wrinkly, soft arms”, the inserted “flurry” above struck “whirlwind”, “awakened” after struck “opened”, and the closing “The day [struck “her world”] everything had changed”, bringing question-specific answer-set coverage to **175/253 (69.2%)**, completing June 2023 P1 and P1R at 11/11 each. A candidate score belongs only to the real script Pearson reproduced; no score is claimed for any constructed model. Separate June 2023 Source Booklet PDFs are absent, but the source texts are appended to the QP PDFs (P1 PDF pp. 30–34; P1R PDF pp. 30–33).
