Humans are naturally sentient creatures. So, it is common for them to have the desire to order for freedom, and choice about how they spend their time and their actions. People often argue that people should not be entitled to freedom and choice about how they spend their time and actions. However, I strongly agree with this statement.
Firstly, Humans have a mind of their own. They are neither animals or birds. Every human has a right to their own free will. A person should not be denied freedom and choice. For example, when a person is finally an adult, no individual should tell them which job they should pick because that is not affecting whatever the person picks, it is not going to interfere with the other individual's personal life. Furthermore, if someone was forced to pick a job that they do not enjoy or appreciate, Eventually they will be really disappointed with their choice therefore in some of the worst cases it will worsen the relationship with the person who forced him to pick that job.
Subsequently, Individuals should not blindly follow someone's advice. Instead, they should take multiple opinions of many people. In this way, that person will be free to judge their own choices. For instance, whenever we go on a vacation, visit a new restaurant, or maybe even order food in the said restaurant. No person, should force their choices. Instead of forcing they should give opinions on where to go, what to order etc. This way our actions will not affect them and their actions will not cause problems for us.
Lastly, whenever we reach a very important point which can shape our future career. Our parents, relatives should not interfere with actions like these. Furthermore, they should support our decision whether they like it or dislike it. we get forced to pick between being an engineer or doctor. But, our goal is being an artist or songwriter. It is very common for parents to want their children become engineers or doctors. But they never listen to what the son/daughter wants to become. Usually when children are made pressured into picking career paths like this, It often causes mental stress disorders like depression.
On the contrary, Parents should build up good habits to control some aspects of their child's lives. Children should not have complete freedom at the ages of 9-12 because they might do something wrong or cause harm to someone else's son/daughter. Full freedom means discord and chaos. Many kids grow up without parents and nobody tells them what to do and what not. As a result, they think every deed will not have consequences. This free will often ends up being the sole reason of their down fall.
In conclusion, I strongly agree that people should be given free will in their choices and action. But a limit should exist in order to avoid chaos and interference in other people's lives.
