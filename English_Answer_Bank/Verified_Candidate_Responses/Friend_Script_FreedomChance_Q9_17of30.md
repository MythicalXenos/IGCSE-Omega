# Marked script — freedom and choice, Q9. 12/20 AO4 + 5/10 AO5 = 17/30

Source: "English (2).pdf", 28 pages, a real candidate answer booklet from an IGCSE English
Language B Paper 1 examination, with the examiner's marks written in the Section C box on page 20:
`12 SeCAO4` / `5 SeCAO5`, total 17 on the final page.

Prompt (page 21, verbatim):
**9 'People are entitled to freedom and choice about how they spend their time and what they do,
as long as it doesn't harm others.' To what extent do you agree with this statement?**

This is the fifth marked Section C script in the archive and the first with a full mark breakdown
visible. It is worth more than any model answer because the examiner's number is on it.

## Stats
490 words. Paragraphs: 58 / 120 / 89 / 102 / 88 / 33.
The QP itself prints "Write approximately 400 words".

## Why 12/20 (Level 3) and not 16 (Level 4)
Level 3 = "communicates clearly, clear sense of purpose, appropriate form, tone and register".
Level 4 = "secure realisation of purpose... effective form, tone and register".

1. THE PROMPT'S OWN CONDITION IS IGNORED.
   "...as long as it doesn't harm others." That subordinate clause IS the answer to the question.
   The candidate never once refers to it, then "discovers" a limit for himself in paragraph 6
   ("a limit should exist") as though it were his own conclusion. The marks for the whole essay
   were sitting in the half of the sentence he did not read.

2. THE POSITION IS CONTRADICTED.
   P1: "I strongly agree." P5: parents must control children aged 9-12, "full freedom means
   discord and chaos". P6: "a limit should exist". If a limit must exist, the candidate does not
   agree with unlimited entitlement - and never reconciles the two.

3. FIVE REASONS, NO TURN.
   Firstly / Subsequently / Lastly / On the contrary. Four paragraphs agree with the statement and
   the fifth disagrees. That is not a discussion, it is a list, and the reversal is not answered.

4. EVERY EXAMPLE IS A HYPOTHETICAL, NONE IS A SCENE.
   "when a person is finally an adult"; "whenever we go on a vacation, visit a new restaurant";
   "being an engineer or doctor". No person, no place, no hour. Nothing the examiner can see.

5. PARAGRAPH 3 HAS NO MAIN CLAUSE.
   "For instance, whenever we go on a vacation, visit a new restaurant, or maybe even order food
   in the said restaurant." That is a fragment. In a paragraph that is supposed to be carrying an
   argument.

6. PARAGRAPH 5 IS A DIFFERENT ESSAY.
   Parenting, discipline, absent parents, delinquency. It argues about how children should be
   raised, not about whether people are entitled to make their own choices.

## Why 5/10 (bottom of Level 3) on accuracy
Roughly twenty errors in 490 words. Named list:
- "neither animals or birds" -> neither ... nor
- "Ultimately/Eventually" capitalised mid-sentence after a comma: "Eventually they will be"
- "Subsequently" used as a connector for a new reason; it means "later"
- "Individuals" capitalised mid-sentence: "advice. Instead," / "choice. Individuals"
- Sentence fragment, P3, as above
- "No person, should force" - comma between subject and verb
- "Lastly, whenever we reach a very important point which can shape our future career." - fragment
- "Our parents, relatives should not interfere" - needs "and"
- "we get forced" at sentence start with a lower-case w
- "between being an engineer or doctor" -> between being an engineer or a doctor
- "to want their children become" -> children to become
- "It often causes" - capital after comma
- "the sole reason of their down fall" -> reason for their downfall
- "in their choices and action" -> actions
- "to the ages of 9-12" -> at the ages
- "order for freedom" - garbled phrase
- "Firstly, Humans" - capital mid-sentence
Level 3 descriptor: "some control of punctuation and some accurate paragraphing... communicate
successfully even if there were errors." That is exactly this script.

## The transferable lesson, in one line
Read the WHOLE prompt. Half the marks in this question were inside "as long as it doesn't harm
others", and the candidate wrote 490 words without touching it.
