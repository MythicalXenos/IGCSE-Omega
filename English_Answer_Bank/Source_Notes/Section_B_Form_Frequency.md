Extracted 3 Nov 2026 from every *QP.pdf in the 4EB1 archive. Verbatim question stems.

## The claim being checked
"Website contributions only come on R papers." FALSE.

Contribution on a standard P1: **Jan 2022 P1** ("A website for travellers is looking for
contributions on the topic of places to stay") and **Jun 2022 P1** ("A website is asking for
contributions about the importance of clear written communication").
Contribution on an R paper: **2020 P1R** (extreme weather).

So: two of three contributions on record are on STANDARD papers.

## What is actually true
- Contributions appear on both. The friend's rule is wrong.
- R papers skew to the TALK: half of all R papers on record are talks, against 36% of standard
  papers. So the instinct behind the claim is roughly right in direction, wrong in kind.
- The only form seen ONCE is the letter to a newspaper (Jun 2019 P1 only) — and it is a standard
  paper, not an R.
- The most recent standard papers (Nov 2024, Jun 2026) are both school/college website ARTICLES.

## Correction to an earlier statement of mine
An earlier count in this session said "13 Section B questions" and included the June 2026 P2
paper, which is a separate paper with the same structure. Excluding it, the standard P1 record is
14 papers. The form counts above replace the earlier ones.

## Practical conclusion
Prepare all five. On the record, a standard paper gives:
- a talk or a letter to a friend 8 times in 14 (57%)
- an article 3 times in 14 (21%)
- a contribution 2 times in 14 (14%)
- a letter to a newspaper 1 time in 14 (7%)
Knowing the contribution costs about ten minutes to learn. Not knowing it costs the AO4 marks for
the whole question if it appears.
