# OCR-route extracts from the June 2025 examiner reports — raw material, not transcripts

**Status: NOT verified, NOT counted in the authentic-response index (which stays at 92).** These are the reproduced-script pages of the June 2025 P1 and P1R reports as they come through Pearson's own text layer over the scanned scripts. The layer is OCR of handwriting and it is visibly noisy, so nothing here may be quoted in an answer set as candidate wording, and no mark is claimed for any wording below.

**Why only these scripts.** Pearson's OCR layer over reproduced scripts is partial: in both June 2025 reports it exists for the Section A questions on printed pp. 18–30 and is **absent** for the Q8 and Section C script pages (the reader route shows printed pp. 54–57 as bare page numbers with no text). Since the workspace cannot download Pearson PDFs (see `Report_Access_Routes.md` §1), these are the only reproduced scripts reachable from here at all.

**What the noise looks like.** OCR artefacts are marked with `[?]` only where the reading is genuinely doubtful; obvious artefacts are shown as printed so the reader can see the layer's quality. Examiner annotations sometimes merge into the candidate's sentences — where that has happened the segment is bracketed `[[…]]` and flagged.
