## Paper structure and priority

- **Section A, Q1–Q7 /40:** retrieval Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5; analysis Q3 and Q6; comparison Q7.
- **Section B, Q8 /30:** reading-to-writing, assessed through AO1, AO4 and AO5.
- **Section C, answer one of Q9/Q10/Q11 /30:** discursive/argumentative, narrative, or descriptive writing.
- Section A’s Q3/Q6/Q7 account for 35/40 marks, while the four retrieval questions together account for 5/40. Prioritise analysis and comparison without neglecting fast, accurate retrieval.

## Durable evidence contract

Every bank entry must use one of these labels:

1. **`[VERIFIED CANDIDATE EXEMPLAR]`** — Pearson’s report confirms the exact candidate response and published score; wording is checked against the source facsimile. Preserve uncertainty and incomplete excerpts.
2. **`[OFFICIAL CANDIDATE SCRIPT LOCATOR]`** — Pearson reproduces an authentic candidate response and publishes its score/level and printed judgement-page locator, but wording has not been transcribed or visually checked. A locator is not a transcript; OCR is locator evidence only.
3. **`[OFFICIAL MARK-SCHEME WORKED ANSWER]`** — written from official indicative content/mark scheme; not a scored student script.
4. **`[CONSTRUCTED GRADE 9 TARGET]`** — original practice target; not official, authentic or examiner-marked and has no claimed score.

If a PDF text route mangles a scanned script, preserve the official URL, report page, level and score when verified, but do **not** repair OCR by guessing, paraphrase the script as authentic, or claim to have transcribed it. Exact script wording needs visual verification against the official report. Challenge unsupported claims and check the strongest counter-interpretation before treating advice as a rule.

For Q3/Q6/Q7, use the exact paper question, source text and matching mark scheme. Do not analyse an invented extract. For a user attempt, mark against the correct QP/MS/ER; quote exact unclear handwriting before asking for clarification. Coach with specific prompts and surgical feedback, letting the learner attempt first unless they explicitly ask for a full model.

## Current specification note for Q8

Pearson’s 4 August 2025 update says that from **November 2025 onward**, Q8 uses only four Issue 4 text types:

- article for a school/college magazine or website;
- speech/talk to peers;
- contribution to a website;
- letter.

Older Q8 forms can still be useful reading practice, but do not assume those forms remain available in current papers. Source links and form guidance are in the examiner research file and the official exemplar index.
