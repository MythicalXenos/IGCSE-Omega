## Storage policy

No QP, Source Booklet, mark-scheme or examiner-report PDFs are duplicated inside this answer-bank folder. The current checkout has **48 individual 4EB1 PDFs** in `Paperlords_Archive/IGCSE/4EB1_English_Language_B/` (23 QPs, 23 MSs and the June 2023 P1/P1R examiner reports). The June 2026 P1/P2 MS archive filenames are reversed relative to their internal cover codes; `Paperlords_QP_MS_ER_Pairings.csv` now maps each QP to the MS with the matching cover/code and content, while preserving the original PDF names and bytes. See `Paperlords_Archive/README.md` and `Answer_Sets/June_2026_P1_Section_A.md`. The searchable index and pairing metadata are also present. The single full-collection ZIP is absent, but the 4EB1 PDFs are available at their archive-relative paths. Separate June 2023 Source Booklet PDFs are not present, but the Source Booklet texts are appended to the local QP PDFs (P1 PDF pp. 30–34 / printed pp. 2–6; P1R PDF pp. 30–33 / printed pp. 2–5). Candidate script references are transcribed as written and are not presented as source-verified quotations unless a specific cross-check is recorded. June 2019, January 2020, November 2020, November 2021, January 2022, June 2022, November 2023 and June 2024–2025 examiner-report PDFs cited in the notes are linked official Pearson sources, not local files. The June 2019 P1R extracted publication-code line omits “R” despite its P1R URL/title; direct report-image requests failed, so retain this discrepancy as unresolved until a facsimile is visually available. No public examiner report or score-bearing response was found for the May/June 2021 optional unseen assessment; Pearson’s secure Overview Document was not accessed, so this is a public-source gap rather than proof of non-existence. Use the local PDFs for visual/page-image checks where present; do not infer that a linked ER is locally stored.

## Evidence policy

- `[VERIFIED CANDIDATE EXEMPLAR]` is reserved for candidate wording checked against the Pearson facsimile, with the published score, session, paper, question and printed report page recorded. Preserve uncertainty and incomplete excerpts.
- `[OFFICIAL CANDIDATE SCRIPT LOCATOR]` means Pearson reproduces an authentic response and publishes its score/level and judgement-page locator, but exact wording has not been transcribed or visually checked. OCR is locator evidence only; a locator is not a transcript.
- `[OFFICIAL MARK-SCHEME WORKED ANSWER]` means an answer developed from Pearson’s indicative content/mark scheme; it is not a scored candidate script.
- `[CONSTRUCTED GRADE 9 TARGET]` means an original answer to an exact past-paper task. It is not official, authentic or examiner-marked and carries no claimed score; “target” is aspirational, not a guaranteed grade.
- Reports may contain scanned candidate scripts that web text extraction corrupts. Scores and examiner comments can be logged when verified, but exact candidate wording is not reconstructed from bad OCR. A transcript is added only after visual checking against the facsimile; uncertain, crossed-out or excerpted wording is flagged, and the source PDF/page locator is retained.
- The archive has two 4EB1 examiner reports (June 2023 P1 and P1R). Additional report links are official Pearson online sources and are not duplicated locally.
- Pearson’s 4 August 2025 update limits Section B Q8 text types from November 2025 onward to article for a school/college magazine or website, speech/talk to peers, contribution to a website, and letter. Older papers remain useful for reading practice, but their Q8 forms are not automatically current.
