## Exact QP prompt — Q8 (printed p. 15)

> Use ideas from BOTH Text One and Text Two in the Source Booklet to answer this question.
>
> Give a talk to your peers about how successful people can be an inspiration.
>
> You should include:
> - the challenges they faced
> - what influenced them
> - advice on how to be successful.
>
> Write the text of your talk.
>
> Think carefully about the purpose of your talk and the audience for whom it is intended.
>
> (30)

## Matching MS criteria

**Indicative content (MS p. 13):** Use a suitable register for a talk to peers and address all three areas. Pearson lists examples, not a closed set, and says to reward all valid points.

- **Challenges they faced:** little money; lack of correct knowledge; being young; a long time to succeed; no access to online advertising; lack of appropriate education; lack of confidence; stereotyping; lack of guidance.
- **What influenced them:** personal experience; education; family and friends; helping people achieve; overcoming obstacles; the media; seeing benefits to society.
- **Advice on how to be successful:** study/get an education; accept that you may change direction; be determined; believe in yourself; do not be disheartened; stay humble rather than arrogant; take risks.

**Assessment objectives:**

- **AO1, 10 marks (MS p. 14):** Level 5 is 9–10. It requires apt and persuasive selection/interpretation, a wide range of relevant points and perceptive, well-focused comments with references to ideas.
- **AO4, 12 marks (MS p. 15):** Level 4 is 8–10; Level 5 is 11–12. Level 4 requires successful communication, secure awareness of purpose/audience, and effective form, tone and register. Level 5 requires perceptive, subtle communication and a sharply focused, sophisticated realisation of the task.
- **AO5, 8 marks (MS p. 16):** Level 4 is the highest printed band, at 7–8 marks; there is no Level 5 in this grid. It requires complex ideas, coherent structural/grammatical control, strategically extensive vocabulary, accurate punctuation and selective sentence choices.
