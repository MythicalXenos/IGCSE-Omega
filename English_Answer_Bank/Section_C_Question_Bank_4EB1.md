# Section C question bank — every 4EB1 Paper 1 in the local archive

Extracted directly from `Paperlords_Archive/IGCSE/4EB1_English_Language_B/*/*P1_QP.pdf`, the "SECTION C" page of each paper. Verbatim.

Standing instructions on every paper: **"Answer ONE question from this section." / "You should spend 1 hour on your chosen question." / "Write approximately 400 words."** Marks: 30 (AO4 20 + AO5 10, MS p.22–23 — one grid shared by Q9, Q10 and Q11).

## The pattern that matters

**Q9 is a short quotable claim followed by "Discuss." or "To what extent do you agree…?" — on all fourteen papers.** There is no paper in the archive where Q9 is a narrative or a description. A candidate who only writes discursively will always have exactly one option, and it will always look like this.

Q10 is always "Write a story (true or imaginary) entitled '…'". Q11 is always "Describe …".

## Evidence gap, stated plainly

The bank holds **two authentic Q9 scripts, both 17/30** (June 2023 P1 and P1R): AO4 11/20 Level 3, AO5 6/10 Level 3 in both cases. **There is no authentic Q9 script above 17 in the archive.** Any claim about what a 25+/30 Q9 looks like cannot be evidenced from this archive and should be treated as inference from the grid, not as fact.

The nearest usable calibration is the **26/30 Q11** and **30/30 Q10** scripts, because AO4 and AO5 use one grid across Q9/10/11 (MS p.22–23). Reading them shows what "perceptive and subtle… sharply focused on purpose" (AO4 17–20) and "manipulates complex ideas… punctuates with accuracy to aid emphasis" (AO5 9–10) look like in practice. That transfer is legitimate on the grid and should be labelled as a cross-application when used.

## What the two 17/30 anchors actually do

Both are competent, coherent, complete answers. Both do the same three things:

1. **Pick a side weakly, then hedge.** P1 Q9: *"Some people would agree… but I understand why some wouldn't."* P1R Q9: *"I partly do agree with this statement."* The opening commits to nothing.
2. **List opinions instead of advancing an argument.** Each paragraph is a new topic rather than a development of the last. P1 Q9 moves vitamin D → weather → being indoors → social anxiety → what "some may say" → conclusion.
3. **End by restating the opening.** P1 Q9's conclusion: *"my opinions on '…' are that it is false, those are not the only things you need to be happy."* No new thought, no resolution of the tension raised in the middle.

Both also contradict themselves inside a few sentences. P1R Q9: *"without it you are nothing"* … then three lines later *"Although I do agree money isnt important."*

**Pearson's own AO4 comment on P1R Q9 notes that the script "supports points made with references to research and the use of statistics."** That is credit for technique at Level 3 — it is not verification of the figures, and the script's Mansa Musa quotation and its "University of Ohio 2014" / "Forbes 2012" statistics are candidate assertions with no source in the paper. Invented research did not lift either script above Level 3. Do not read that as an instruction to invent evidence.
