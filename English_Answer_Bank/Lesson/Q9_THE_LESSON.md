Two consequences, both practical:

1. **The two marks are one mark.** You cannot grind out AO5 separately with a proofread while the
   argument stays flat. In practice the paper gives you either 19+10 or 11+6.
2. **There is no 19–23.** The eight scores are 15, 17, 17, 18, 24, 26, 28, 29. The middle of the range
   is empty. Answers either work as a piece of writing or they don't; there is very little half-credit.

## 3. The five decisions in the exam

**Decision 1 — shape (30 seconds).** Two worlds / four areas / both sides.
**Decision 2 — material (2 minutes).** Eight-place generator; pick four.
**Decision 3 — first two sentences (2 minutes).** Four proven openings; question words by sentence two.
**Decision 4 — the turn in every paragraph.** Six moves.
**Decision 5 — landing and accuracy (3 minutes).** Three endings; the twelve-item AO5 list.
