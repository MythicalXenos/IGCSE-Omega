# 4EB1 Completion Map

**Updated: 2026-10-28** · Canonical scope and progress snapshot for the full English Language B answer bank.

## Completion standard

The scope is **all 23 archived QP/MS pairs and all 253 question-option entries**, across Sections A, B and C. Every paper has Q1–Q8 plus the three separate Section C options Q9, Q10 and Q11. Q9–Q11 are three distinct bank entries even though a candidate answers one.

An entry is complete only when it has:

1. the exact question prompt, session, component and QP page;
2. the relevant QP source-text location for reading questions;
3. its matching official mark-scheme requirements, including accepted alternatives for retrieval questions;
4. a usable answer or answer key that addresses that exact task; and
5. a clear evidence label. Original/constructed answers are never described as Pearson-marked and carry no invented score.

A score/page locator, a selected model in the guide, or a question inventory row **does not** count as a complete answer set. Authentic Pearson responses, MS-based worked answers and constructed practice targets remain distinct evidence types. A full-mark real script for every question is not a condition of completion; where Pearson has not published one, record the source limitation rather than inventing an exemplar.

## Validation and update rule

When a batch is ready to count as complete:

1. cross-check the exact QP wording, QP page, source text (if any) and matching MS;
2. verify every accepted alternative or required point against the MS;
3. distinguish official transcript, locator, MS-based worked answer and constructed target;
4. add only qualifying entries to `Answer_Sets/coverage.csv` and update the section log in `Answer_Sets/README.md`;
5. rebuild `4EB1_Question_Type_Inventory.xlsx` and `4EB1_Exemplar_and_Model_Answer_Guide.docx` when their source files change;
6. validate the total and per-paper counts against the inventory and check only intended source files in Git. Keep user-modified uploads unstaged.
